A · Shortcuts in the empty You receive card

Once You pay has a token, You receive offers where it can go. The shortcuts go away once a token is picked; tap the receive token to bring them back.

B · "Move across networks" row in the picker

The form stays as it is today. The receive picker opens on the pay token's network, with the same token on other networks in a row above the list.

Static prototype. Prices, balances, rates and fees are illustrative. Which networks a token can reach follows the app on 2026-11-16: SUPPORTED_CROSS_SWAP_NETWORKS in swap.constants.ts, the token groups in env/tokens/groups, OneSec only bringing tokens back to their own network (ONESEC_UNWRAP_ONLY), and NEAR Intents taking Bitcoin to and from EVM and Solana (the major tokens are assumed here).
